About

An eye for visuals. A mind for interaction.

I’m Helen, a UI/UX designer with over six years of experience across visual, web, and interface design. I started with marketing and e-commerce websites; my recent work focuses on coupon redemption, cross-device wayfinding, and museum AR interfaces.

  • Start with where and how it’s usedMy work spans phones, desktop browsers, and 43‑inch kiosks. I consider viewing distance, touch reach, and text length across languages when deciding how to arrange information and controls.
  • Turn rules into flows and statesI translate feature requirements into interaction flows and interface states, including confirmations, completion, and expiry, so each action has clear feedback.
  • Design for how it gets builtI document shared components and UI guidelines in Figma and build responsive pages in HTML/CSS. On the wayfinding and AR projects, I check interfaces on real devices and on site, then adjust text, prompts, and controls based on what I find.

Let’s make things clearer, together.

  • Prototyping: National Museum of History, lantern game flow and prototype
  • Visual Design: GOMAJI Carnival City, key visual and web guidelines
  • HTML / CSS: MamyPoko, guidelines, build, and review

Yuanbenshan | LINE LIFF invoice registration

I built the screens and form validation for this invoice registration app within LINE (LIFF), including a preview of uploaded photos. It supports paper invoices, electronic invoices, and Taiwan’s mobile barcode ID for storing electronic invoices.

What you can try

  • Screens for each registration method: e-invoice, paper invoice, and mobile barcode carrier
  • Field validation and hints
  • A live preview after uploading an invoice photo

The demo is in Chinese, as delivered, and shows the interface only; submissions aren’t verified or stored.

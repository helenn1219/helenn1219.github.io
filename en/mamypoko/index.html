MamyPoko
LINE Coupons

  • UI/UX design
  • UI guidelines
  • HTML/CSS implementation

MamyPoko, a Unicharm brand, offers digital coupons through a web app inside the LINE messaging app. Shoppers can claim coupons and redeem them in participating stores or online. The shopper-facing pages display campaign content managed through a content management system (CMS).

Role
UI/UX design, UI guidelines, HTML/CSS implementation
Timeline
2020 · about 6 months
Platform
Web app within LINE (LIFF)
Worked with
Client, PM, CMS designer, engineers

What I did

I designed the shopper-facing screens and defined text styles, character limits, and layout rules. I implemented all front-end pages in responsive HTML/CSS and added some interactions with jQuery.

The client and PM defined the redemption rules and flows; another designer handled the CMS. I worked with that designer and the engineers to align the CMS settings with how content appeared on the shopper-facing pages.

01 · FLOW

A shared flow for in-store and online redemption

Both redemption paths share the same starting steps. Shoppers open the campaign page from LINE, claim a coupon, then select it in My Coupons and choose where to redeem it. Some campaigns also require basic details before a coupon can be claimed.

Shared steps
  1. Open from LINE
  2. Claim a coupon
  3. My Coupons
  4. Choose a channel
Separate steps for in-store and online use

In store

  1. Confirm redemption to start the 30-minute countdown
  2. Show the barcode and countdown
  3. Store staff tap “Confirm redemption”

If store staff confirm redemption within 30 minutes, the coupon is marked as redeemed. Otherwise, it expires.

Online

  1. View the retailer’s discount code
  2. Open the retailer’s site

02 · REDEMPTION

Making redemption details easy for store staff to read

At checkout, store staff need to scan the barcode and check the remaining redemption time on the shopper’s phone. I gave the barcode and countdown visual priority, and positioned the button and note to make clear that store staff should confirm redemption.

03 · STATES

Showing coupon status and redemption results

My Coupons groups coupons under Available, Used, and Expired tabs. Separate result screens confirm redemption with a date or explain that a coupon has expired. Both include customer service hours and a phone number. Section 02 shows the screen used during in-store redemption.

Manage coupons
Results

If redemption fails, the interface prompts the shopper to try again. The “Already claimed” message appears when claiming a coupon; it is separate from the redemption results.

04 · BUILD & QA

Defining display rules for CMS content

I defined font-size options, character limits, and rules for text wrapping and overflow so campaign content would fit the page layouts. I checked how each text size appeared in the interface and aligned the corresponding CMS settings with the CMS designer. I then implemented the agreed text styles in HTML/CSS for engineering integration.

After implementing the pages and jQuery interactions, I checked the claiming and redemption flows in the test environment. I reviewed page transitions and checked that content configured in the CMS displayed as intended.

05 · OUTCOME

A coupon interface for in-store and online use

The project took approximately six months, with the shopper-facing interface and CMS delivered together. My work covered the coupon claim flow, My Coupons, in-store and online redemption, and result screens, including the HTML/CSS implementation of all front-end pages.

Reflection

The front-end layout depended on what campaign editors could enter in the CMS. Defining text limits and display rules alongside the CMS settings was therefore part of the interface design, not just an implementation detail.
